Privacy policy and terms

VITALS 2.0, the student health monitor. Last updated 21 November 2026.

What VITALS 2.0 is

VITALS 2.0 is an educational student health tool for schools. It helps students, teachers and school nurses keep an eye on everyday health: readings from a sensor, hydration, sleep, hygiene and first aid. It is a prototype, not a medical device, and it gives general information, not a diagnosis.

In an emergency, tell a teacher or the school nurse straight away and call your local emergency number. Do not wait for VITALS 2.0.

Students under 18

Most people using VITALS 2.0 are school students. A student should use it with the agreement of a parent or carer and their school. Parents and carers can ask to see, change or delete their child's data at any time (see "Your choices" below).

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and name. If you sign in with Google, Google gives us only your name and email address. We never see your Google password.
  • Your student card: the name, role in class, class and school-year end date you type in, and a student ID we generate.
  • Your readings: heart rate, blood oxygen, ECG, room temperature and humidity from your sensor or the demo device, the daily log (sleep, water, exercise) and the personal baselines you enter.
  • Student Link: the class you create or join, check-ins and notes you post to your class log.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or anything from your phone beyond what you type in or the sensor sends.

Who can see it

  • You can see all of your own data.
  • Your teacher and school nurse (accounts the school has given that role) can see a short status summary: your name, role in class, status (green, amber or red), a few key vitals and when you last synced. They cannot see your full reading history.
  • Classmates in a Student Link class you join see the same short summary and the class log. You only join a class by choosing to.

The database enforces these rules, not just the website.

Chatbots

The chatbots answer school health questions. When you are online, a question you type may be sent to an AI service to write the answer; we do not store your questions. Otherwise answers come from a library built into the site. Don't type private details like your full name, address or passwords into a chatbot.

Where it is kept

Account data is kept in Supabase. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. VITALS 2.0 is separate from the original VITALS: accounts and data are not shared between them. We do not sell your data, show ads or use trackers.

Your choices

You can edit your student card and baselines on the account page at any time, and leave a Student Link class whenever you like. To have your account and all its data deleted, or if a parent or carer wants to see or delete a child's data, ask your school or the VITALS 2.0 team and we will delete it.

Terms of use

VITALS 2.0 is provided as it is, for learning and demonstration, with no warranty. Do not rely on it for medical decisions; always follow your school's health policy and a doctor's advice. Use it respectfully and lawfully, and do not try to see other people's data. We may change or stop the service at any time.
