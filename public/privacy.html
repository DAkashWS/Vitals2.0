Privacy policy and terms

VITALS, by team Mission 61:4, a NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 project. Last updated 15 November 2026.

What VITALS is

VITALS is an educational crew health monitor built for a hackathon. It is a prototype, not a medical device, and it gives general information, not a diagnosis. In an emergency, contact a real doctor or emergency services.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and name. If you sign in with Google, Google gives us only your email address and name. We never see your Google password.
  • Your crew card: the job, mission and mission end date you type in, and a crew number we generate.
  • Your readings: the heart rate, blood oxygen, ECG, room and daily-log values from your sensor or the demo device, and the personal baselines you enter.

Readings are saved on your own device first. When you are signed in and online, they are uploaded to your account so they follow you to other devices.

Who can see it

Your readings are visible only to you. A short status summary (name, job, status and a few key vitals) is also visible to crew members with a commander or flight-surgeon role, and to members of a crew you join in Crew Link. The database enforces this, not the website.

Chatbots

When you are online, a question you type into a chatbot is sent to our endpoint on Cloudflare, which asks an AI model to write the answer. We do not store your questions. Offline, answers come from a library on your device and nothing is sent.

Where it is kept

Account data is kept in Supabase. Files and the chatbot endpoint are hosted on Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages. We do not sell your data, show ads or use trackers.

Your choices

You can edit your crew card and baselines on the account page at any time. To have your account and data deleted, ask the Mission 61:4 team through the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 team page.

Terms of use

VITALS is provided as it is, for learning and demonstration, with no warranty. Do not rely on it for medical decisions. Use it lawfully and do not try to access other people's data. We may change or stop the service at any time.
