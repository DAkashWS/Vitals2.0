---
title: Health on the Moon and Mars
source: NASA (Artemis and Mars exploration); Wikipedia, "Lunar soil"
---

## Is moon dust dangerous?

Yes. Lunar dust is very fine and sharp, because there's no wind or water on the Moon to smooth the grains. It sticks to everything, wears down seals and suits, and can irritate the eyes, nose and lungs. Apollo 17 astronaut Harrison Schmitt described a "lunar hay fever" after it got into the cabin. Keep dust out of the habitat, and report a cough, sneezing or sore eyes after moonwalks.

## What does moon dust smell like?

Apollo astronauts said lunar dust brought into the cabin smelled like spent gunpowder. Even if it smells harmless, avoid breathing it in.

## Is Mars dust dangerous?

Mars dust is fine like lunar dust, and Martian soil contains chemicals called perchlorates, which can harm the thyroid gland if taken in. Dust storms on Mars can also cover the whole planet for weeks, blocking sunlight and coating equipment. Future crews will need to keep Martian dust out of the habitat and away from food and water.

## How strong is gravity on the Moon and Mars?

The Moon's gravity is about one sixth of Earth's, and Mars's is about three eighths. That's more than in orbit, so bones, muscles and balance get some load, but much less than on Earth. Scientists don't yet know how much partial gravity protects the body over a long stay.

## How long do messages take to reach Mars?

Radio signals travel at the speed of light, but Mars is so far away that a message takes about 3 to 22 minutes to arrive, depending on where Earth and Mars are in their orbits. A question and its answer can take up to about 45 minutes. When Mars is behind the Sun, contact can be cut for about two weeks. Crews must be able to handle medical problems on their own.

## How long does a mission to Mars take?

With today's rockets, the trip takes roughly six to nine months each way. Because Earth and Mars must line up for the return trip, a full mission could last two to three years, including a long stay on the surface.

## How long does it take to get to the Moon?

A trip to the Moon takes about three days. The Moon is about 384,000 kilometres from Earth, and messages take just over a second each way, so mission control can still talk with the crew almost in real time.
